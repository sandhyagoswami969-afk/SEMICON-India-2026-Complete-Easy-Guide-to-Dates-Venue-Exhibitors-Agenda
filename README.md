India’s semiconductor story is moving from ambition to execution—and SEMICON India 2026 arrives at an important moment in that journey.
Scheduled for September 17–19, 2026, the fifth edition of SEMICON India will bring semiconductor manufacturers, equipment suppliers, chip designers, policymakers, researchers, startups, investors and [electronics companies together](https://edmyra.com/) at Yashobhoomi (India International Convention & Expo Centre), New Delhi. The official theme for this year is “Silicon to Systems: Building the Ecosystem.”

The event is also significantly larger than previous editions. Official announcements point to 600+ exhibitors, including around 300 international companies, more than 150 speakers, 15,000 square metres of exhibition space and participation from more than 40 countries.

If you are planning to attend SEMICON India 2026—or simply want to understand what the event means for India’s semiconductor industry—here is a complete guide.
When Is SEMICON India 2026?
SEMICON India 2026 takes place from Thursday, September 17, to Saturday, September 19, 2026.

The event begins with its inauguration on September 17. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to inaugurate the fifth edition at Yashobhoomi.

For visitors, that means the three-day event offers a combination of exhibition areas, conference sessions, technical discussions, networking opportunities and special programmes.

One important point for attendees: September 17 is not simply an exhibition day. It also includes the opening programme and major policy and keynote sessions, making the first day particularly relevant for people interested in the broader direction of India’s semiconductor industry.

Where Is SEMICON India 2026 Being Held?
The venue is Yashobhoomi (India International Convention & Expo Centre), New Delhi, in the Dwarka area.

The exhibition itself is being hosted across Hall 1 and Hall 2.

Yashobhoomi is a natural fit for an event of this scale because SEMICON India has expanded considerably. With hundreds of exhibitors and thousands of square metres of exhibition space, visitors should expect a large show floor rather than a small conference-style exhibition.

If you’re attending, it is worth checking the official floor plan before arriving. Knowing which hall or booth you want to visit can save considerable time once you are inside the venue.

Because the exhibitor list is extensive, it is more useful to think about exhibitors by category.

1. Semiconductor Equipment
Equipment companies are an important part of the show because semiconductor manufacturing requires highly specialised machinery at virtually every stage.

Visitors can expect companies involved in wafer processing, inspection, metrology, testing, assembly and other manufacturing technologies.

2. Materials and Chemicals
Semiconductor manufacturing depends on an enormous supporting ecosystem of materials, gases, chemicals and specialised components.

SEMICON India 2026 has dedicated exhibition areas for these suppliers, highlighting the growing importance of the upstream supply chain.

3. Chip Design and IP
India has traditionally been strong in semiconductor design and engineering talent. Design and IP companies therefore remain an important part of the ecosystem.

This is also where visitors interested in chip architecture, EDA, embedded systems and semiconductor startups will find relevant conversations.

4. Packaging and Testing
Advanced packaging is becoming increasingly important as chip architectures become more complex.

SEMICON India 2026 has dedicated attention to packaging and advanced packaging, including a conference session specifically focused on the subject.

5. Electronics and Systems
The event goes beyond chips themselves.

Electronics components and systems manufacturing form another major part of the exhibition, reflecting the connection between semiconductor production and finished electronic products.

SEMICON India 2026 Agenda
The conference programme combines policy discussions, keynotes, technical sessions, panels and industry conversations.

The official Programme at a Glance provides a day-wise schedule, although individual sessions and timings can be updated as the event approaches.

Day 1: September 17
The opening day begins with the Grand Inaugural Session, followed by the exhibition opening.

The published agenda includes a “Lighting the Vision: Keynote & Policy Makers Session”, followed by sessions covering areas such as NextGen Manufacturing and Packaging.

For executives, investors and policymakers, the first day is particularly relevant because it connects the technology discussions with India’s broader semiconductor strategy.

Day 2: September 18
The second day moves deeper into industry and [technology discussions](https://edmyra.com/).

Conference tracks cover topics across semiconductor manufacturing, advanced packaging, electronics, AI, supply-chain resilience, sustainability and related technologies.

The programme also incorporates international participation and industry-led discussions, allowing visitors to move beyond government policy and explore practical technology and business questions.

Day 3: September 19
The final day continues the technical and ecosystem-focused programme while providing opportunities for networking and industry engagement.

For students, startups and professionals, the later days can be particularly useful for spending more time on the exhibition floor and connecting with companies.

Country and State Pavilions
Another feature to watch is the collection of country and state government pavilions.

The latest government announcement says SEMICON India 2026 will feature six country pavilions and 12 state government pavilions.

These pavilions provide a different perspective from individual company booths.

For international participants, they can offer insight into how different markets are positioning themselves within the semiconductor supply chain. For Indian states, the pavilions provide an opportunity to showcase industrial infrastructure, investment opportunities, policies, manufacturing capabilities and talent.

Why SEMICON India 2026 Matters?
The timing of this year’s event is particularly notable.

India has spent the last few years attempting to build a stronger domestic semiconductor ecosystem through government incentives, manufacturing projects, design initiatives and international partnerships.

SEMICON India 2026 arrives as the country attempts to move from announcing projects to building a more complete ecosystem.

SEMI says the event reflects India’s effort to develop capabilities spanning design, manufacturing, packaging, materials, equipment, electronics systems, research and talent development.

The government’s semiconductor programme has also expanded into its next phase, with Semicon 2.0 focusing on areas including chip design, machines and materials, additional fabs, ATMP/OSAT, R&D and talent development.

That makes SEMICON India more than a conventional trade exhibition. It is also a meeting point for companies looking at India’s emerging semiconductor market.

Tips for Visiting SEMICON India 2026
If you’re planning to attend, a little preparation will go a long way.

First, study the exhibitor list. With hundreds of companies participating, trying to visit every booth is unrealistic. Create a shortlist based on your interests.

Second, review the agenda beforehand. Some of the most valuable information may come from conference sessions rather than the exhibition floor.

Third, allow enough time for the venue. Yashobhoomi is a large convention complex, and moving between sessions and exhibition areas can take time.

Fourth, use the event map. The official online exhibitor map provides booth information and can help you plan your route through the exhibition.

Finally, don’t treat SEMICON as only a technology exhibition. Networking is a major part of the experience. Bring business cards or a digital profile, identify companies you want to meet and make the most of the three days.

For anyone following India’s semiconductor industry, September 17–19 at Yashobhoomi, New Delhi is therefore a date worth marking.
